Loan agreement

Please read carefully — this is a binding agreement between you and our lending partner

1. Nature of the advance. The rentAdvance is a short-term credit facility, not a rent waiver. Your landlord is paid the full requested amount today; you owe that amount back to our lending partner, not to your landlord.

2. Service fee. A service fee of 5% of the advance amount is included in your repayment schedule, spread evenly across your chosen repayment period.

3. Repayment. Repayments are collected automatically via Mobile Money on the frequency you selected. Missed repayments may be retried the following day and are reflected on your rent ledger.

4. Late payment. Repayments more than 7 days late may be reported to your landlord and may affect your eligibility for future advances.

5. KYC & data use. The identity documents you provide are shared with our lending partner solely to verify your identity and assess this application, in line with Rwandan data protection law.

6. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda. Disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of Rwandan courts.
